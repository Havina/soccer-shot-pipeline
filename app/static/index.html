How it works

  1. The app trims camera cuts, then the clip runs through the frozen 18‑stage pipeline.
  2. If pitch calibration fails on the shot frame, a local overlay retries nearby frames (not Cell‑40 certified).
  3. If the shooter is ambiguous, the pipeline stops at stage 11 and shows you the candidates on the shot frame.
  4. Your pick triggers a stateless replay. A local overlay can break jersey‑colour ties so striped kits are less likely to fail team assignment.
  5. If the calibrated pose swaps the two pitch ends, a local overlay flips to the near goal before distance, angle and xG are computed.

Frozen sources are hash‑checked at startup. Local overlays never edit those files and are labelled on each job.
